Get more value from these guides

Every article on this hub is written to help you make better travel decisions — and to help you try Rydo, the route-based carpool & seat share app for private travellers in India. Read a guide, run the numbers, then take one action: download the app and match a real corridor.

What you can do in the next 10 minutes

  1. Open the Daily Travel Cost Calculator and estimate your yearly solo spend.
  2. Read one safety checklist so OTP and live tracking become habit.
  3. Download Rydo and create your Rider or Captain profile.
  4. Save your common start and stop locations.
  5. Post or request one ride on a route you already travel.

Why people switch to shared routes

  • Fuel and parking costs keep rising
  • Empty seats waste money every weekday
  • OTP + live tracking improve peace of mind vs informal groups
  • Favourites make recurring office carpools easier
