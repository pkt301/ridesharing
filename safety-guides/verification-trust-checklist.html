Verification & trust checklist

Build trust on Rydo with document verification, ratings, favourites, blocking, and clear communication before every shared ride.

Trust signals to check

  • Completed profile with clear name
  • Ride history and ratings where available
  • Consistent route behaviour (same corridors)
  • Polite, clear messaging about timing and seats

Platform tools

Rydo supports identity/document verification flows for community safety, in-app messaging, OTP start, live tracking, favourites, and blocking. Use them. Report serious issues to support with trip details.

Verification helps trust but does not replace personal judgement. Always follow local laws and never use the platform for commercial taxi activity.

Safety is a process, not a single feature

Apps can provide OTP, live tracking, messaging, and reporting. Personal habits complete the system: public pickups, informed family, verified details, and the willingness to cancel when something feels wrong.

Non-negotiables for every shared ride

  1. Confirm name / vehicle details visually at pickup.
  2. Share or verify the Ride OTP before the trip starts.
  3. Keep live tracking on during active travel.
  4. Use in-app chat for coordination when possible.
  5. Favourite trusted partners; block unsafe behaviour.

Extra caution situations

  • First-time matches
  • Late-night airport or highway trips
  • Heavy rain and low visibility
  • Unfamiliar pickup zones
