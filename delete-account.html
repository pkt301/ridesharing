What gets deleted

When you delete your Rydo account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your profile, name, email, and phone number
  • Ride history and trip records
  • Payment and transaction history
  • Ratings, reviews, and saved preferences
  • Identity verification documents

Before you go

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Any active rides must be completed or cancelled before submitting a deletion request.
