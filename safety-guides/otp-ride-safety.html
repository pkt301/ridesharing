OTP ride safety

Learn how Ride OTP works on Rydo, why captains must verify the 4-digit code, and how riders should share it safely.

On Rydo, captains are asked to enter a 4-digit Ride OTP shared by the rider before starting the trip. This reduces wrong-pickup mistakes and confirms both parties are on the same matched ride.

How OTP works

  1. Rider and captain match on a route and accept the ride.
  2. Rider receives / can view a 4-digit Ride OTP in the app.
  3. At pickup, captain asks for the OTP and enters it to start the trip.
  4. Live tracking continues until the ride is completed or cancelled.
Never share your OTP with someone who is not your matched captain for that ride. Do not post OTPs in public groups.

Best practices

  • Confirm name and vehicle details visually before sharing OTP.
  • Stand in a well-lit, populated pickup spot when possible.
  • If the code fails, cancel and contact support rather than forcing a start.
  • After the trip, you can favourite trusted captains or block problematic users.

Frequently asked questions

What if I forget the OTP?

Open the active ride screen in Rydo to view it again, or message the captain that you need a moment. Do not share screenshots publicly.

Can a trip start without OTP?

Captains should verify OTP before starting. This is a core safety step for matched rides.

Safety is a process, not a single feature

Apps can provide OTP, live tracking, messaging, and reporting. Personal habits complete the system: public pickups, informed family, verified details, and the willingness to cancel when something feels wrong.

Non-negotiables for every shared ride

  1. Confirm name / vehicle details visually at pickup.
  2. Share or verify the Ride OTP before the trip starts.
  3. Keep live tracking on during active travel.
  4. Use in-app chat for coordination when possible.
  5. Favourite trusted partners; block unsafe behaviour.

Extra caution situations

  • First-time matches
  • Late-night airport or highway trips
  • Heavy rain and low visibility
  • Unfamiliar pickup zones
