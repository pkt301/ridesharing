For Captains

Captain Dashboard

Set your route, match passengers, verify with OTP, and manage every trip from one dashboard.

  • ✅
    Accept ride requests

    See passenger details, route, and fuel-share amount — accept or reject instantly.

  • 🔒
    OTP trip start

    Ask the rider for their 4-digit Ride OTP before starting — extra safety on every trip.

  • 📊
    Trip progress tracker

    Accepted → Arrived → Trip started → Completed — clear status at every step.

  • ⭐
    Favourites & contacts

    Save trusted riders, manage blocked users, and build your regular commute network.

How Rydo works

Four simple steps — whether you're riding or driving.

Download & sign up

Create your account as a Rider or Captain and set your route preferences.

Match on route

Riders search a destination; captains see requests on their active route.

Verify & ride

Captain enters the Ride OTP, trip starts, and both sides track live on the map.

Complete & receipt

Mark the trip done, share fuel cost, and download your PDF receipt.
