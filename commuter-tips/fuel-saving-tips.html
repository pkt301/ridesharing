Fuel saving tips

How to improve vehicle mileage: tyre pressure, smooth acceleration, less weight, regular service, and avoid long idling. Save fuel every month.

Fuel prices impact everyone's monthly budget. Follow these practical tips to improve mileage and spend less.

Maintain correct tyre pressure

Under-inflated tyres increase rolling resistance and burn more fuel. Check pressure monthly.

Avoid sudden acceleration

Smooth driving saves fuel. Aggressive starts and hard braking waste energy.

Reduce extra weight

Heavy vehicles consume more fuel. Clear the boot of things you do not need daily.

Service your vehicle regularly

A healthy engine, clean filters, and timely oil changes help performance and efficiency.

Avoid long idling

Turn off the engine during long stops when safe and legal to do so.

Bonus: share the journey

Even with perfect mileage, solo driving still means you pay 100% of the fuel. Sharing seats on the same route splits the cost. Estimate savings with the fuel price impact calculator.

Turn tips into a weekly commute system

Reading tips helps once. Systems help every month. Treat your commute like a small project: measure cost, reduce waste, improve reliability, and protect safety.

A simple weekly routine

  1. Sunday night: Confirm next week's office/college days and departure windows.
  2. Weekdays: Message delays early; keep pickups consistent.
  3. Friday: Glance at fuel spend vs last week.
  4. Monthly: Recalculate with the daily travel cost calculator when fuel prices move.

Money levers that actually work

  • Higher occupancy on long overlapping segments
  • Fewer solo micro-trips
  • Better tyre pressure and smoother driving
  • Parking awareness at office locations
  • Hybrid WFO clustering when available

Who this helps most

Working professionals on fixed corridors, students on campus routes, and captains who already drive with empty seats. If your route is random every day, start by stabilizing timing first — then matching becomes easier.
